02 · The revenue

    
NEAR's revenue now comes out of the dark

    

      
Revenue reaches NEAR's three wallets as withdrawals from intents.near. We matched each payment against transfers out of the private treasury, to the exact yoctoNEAR. Until July there were none. Now most payments start as a transfer out of the treasury, to a fresh account that exists only to pass the money on.

What this settles

      
We started out asking whether private swaps pay NEAR nothing, because the API Terms say the private fee is kept by a private company for its own account. The data does not support that fear. Money is arriving, and revenue per dollar has moved far less than a full leak would cause.

      
What it breaks

      
Wallet receipts still match DefiLlama, within {{wallet_gap}}. But that check now proves only that money arrived. Nothing public links a private swap to a revenue payment. From outside, a payment out of the treasury looks the same whatever paid for it. We have no evidence that it is anything other than fees. Nobody outside can show that it is.

08 · For NEAR

    
The fix is small, and it makes NEAR easier to own

    
Privacy for users and accounts for holders do not conflict. A daily total reveals nobody's trades. We drafted the proposal and built the parts anyone can use today.

    

      
Publish the totals
Each day: private volume, private fees, and the share sent to NEAR's wallets, signed and open to an auditor's check. Read the draft proposal.

      
Say who holds the key
Publish how the treasury key is held and who can use it, and the validator set of the private chain.

      
Put the rule on chain
State what share of the private fee reaches NEAR's wallets, and keep it where holders can see a change.

    

    

      
A weigh station anyone can call
{{contract_status}} reads the treasury straight from intents.near, with no oracle, and warns on chain if a key changes or money leaves fast. Any contract can read it.

      
An open daily feed
Every series here is free to use, with no key: weight, revenue source, flows and everything.

      
A tool for agents
A small server gives AI agents the same numbers, and a live weighing, through the Model Context Protocol. See it.
